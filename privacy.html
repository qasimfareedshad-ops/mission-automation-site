Privacy Policy — Mission Automation

Last updated: October 10, 2026

What this app does

Mission Automation uploads original short-form videos to our own TikTok accounts via TikTok's official Content Posting API. A human reviews and approves every video before upload. Nothing is posted automatically.

Data we collect

TikTok account information provided via TikTok Login: your TikTok open ID and public username, used only to confirm which account authorized the app.

OAuth tokens (access and refresh tokens) issued by TikTok when you authorize the app. These are used solely to upload videos you have approved to your own accounts.

Video files and captions that you explicitly provide for upload.

Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect analytics, device information, location data, contacts, or any data beyond what is listed above. We do not use tracking technologies.

How we use your data

Your data is used for one purpose only: uploading your approved videos to your own TikTok accounts. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party, except TikTok itself as required by the API.

Data storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on a private server with restricted access. Video files are processed for upload and not retained longer than necessary.

Your rights

You may revoke the app's access at any time from your TikTok settings (Settings > Security > Apps and websites). On request, we will delete all stored tokens and data associated with your account.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the app operator directly through the TikTok developer portal listing.
