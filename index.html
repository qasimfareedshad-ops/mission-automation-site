Mission Automation

An AI-powered video production system. Approved scripts become complete, cinematic, platform-ready videos — published to your own channels with your approval at every step.

How it works

  • Script in. You approve the script; the system plans every shot.
  • Video out. AI production generates the scenes, voiceover, and edit.
  • You publish. Every upload — YouTube, Facebook, TikTok — happens only with your explicit approval per video. Nothing posts automatically.

Platforms

Mission Automation publishes to YouTube, Facebook Pages, and TikTok via each platform's official API, using only the permissions needed to upload your approved videos.

Your data

We collect only what publishing requires: your account identity and the videos you approve. No tracking, no analytics, no data sales. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
